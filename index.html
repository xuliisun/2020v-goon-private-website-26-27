About

Year One Starts Now.

Team 2020V - Twenty-Twenty Vision is a brand-new, youth-led VEX Robotics Competition team based in Markham, Ontario. This is our first year as a team - we formed from scratch, and everything you see here we are building for the very first time.

Being new means we start with no robot, no results, and no shortcuts. What we do have is a group that shows up: eleven students splitting the work across mechanical design, electronics, programming, and documentation, learning each subsystem by actually building it.

Our long-term target is the VEX Robotics World Championship. We know rookie teams rarely get there on the first try - so this season is about building a machine we can trust, learning the game properly, and setting a foundation the team can grow on. Every step of it gets a 20/20 effort.

Season

Our First Season, Start to Finish.

Everything below is year one. We'll keep this updated as we go, so anyone following along can see where we actually are - not where we wish we were.

Spring 2026
Team FormedComplete

Eleven students, one team number, zero robots. We registered as 2020V, split into mechanical, programming, and documentation groups, and started learning the program.

May 2026
Game Release & StrategyComplete

Our first-ever game reveal. We read the manual end to end, scored the strategy options, and picked a robot archetype we thought a rookie team could actually build well.

Summer 2026
First BuildIn Progress

Prototyping subsystems for the first time. The drivetrain is running; intake and scoring are mid-iteration. Every version and every failure is going in the notebook.

Fall 2026
Our First CompetitionUpcoming

The big one for a rookie team: pass inspection, play real matches, and find out how much of what we built survives contact with an actual field.

Winter 2027
Provincial ChampionshipStretch Goal

Ontario provincials. Qualifying in a rookie season would be well ahead of where we expect to be - which is exactly why it's on the list.

Beyond
The Long GameGoal

The VEX World Championship is where we want this to end up. Realistically that's a multi-season climb, and year one is about earning the experience to make it possible.

Why Us

What Your Support Builds.

Here's the honest version: 2020V is a first-year team, and first-year teams are the ones that need help most. We're starting without the equipment, the spare parts, or the budget that established programs have built up over years. Every dollar at this stage goes straight into getting a competitive robot onto the field.

What your sponsorship actually pays for: the V5 control system and motors, structural metal and spare parts, tournament registration fees, and travel to events. These are the startup costs that decide whether a rookie team gets a real season or a stalled one.

In exchange you get in on the ground floor. Your logo goes on our very first robot and our first competition banner, and you'll be listed as a founding partner for as long as this team exists. We document everything publicly, so you'll be able to see precisely what your support built. Let's give it a 20/20 effort.
